Whether you love puzzles, idle games or a little adventure, your feedback can help us make our upcoming games more fun and easier to play.

How it works

  1. Apply for upcoming games. Share your Google Play email and Android device details.
  2. Watch your inbox. If there is a suitable testing opportunity, we will email you an invitation and instructions.
  3. Play and share your thoughts. Tell us about bugs, confusing moments and the things you enjoy.

Before you apply

Do I need experience as a tester?

No. Clear, honest feedback is what matters. If you find a bug, tell us what happened and what you were doing at the time.

Will I get access straight away?

Submitting an application does not guarantee a place or immediate access. Testing opportunities depend on each game's development stage and the devices we need to test.

What should I expect from a beta?

Beta versions are works in progress. You may encounter bugs, unfinished features and changes to gameplay. Saved progress may be reset during testing.

What if my email app does not open?

Email fribbegames@outlook.com with the subject “Beta tester application”. Include your Google Play email, device and Android version to apply for upcoming games.

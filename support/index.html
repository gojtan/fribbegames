Before you get in touch

What should I include in a bug report?

The game name, game version, device model, Android version and the steps that caused the issue. Screenshots are helpful. Please avoid including sensitive personal information.

I have a problem with a purchase.

Check that you are using the same Google Play account as when you made the purchase. Use Restore Purchases if that option is available in your game. If you still need help, email the game name and Google Play order reference. Never send card details or passwords.

How do I delete my game data?

Our data deletion page explains how to erase local progress, manage cloud saves and request removal of support correspondence.

Where can I download the games?

Availability depends on each game’s release and testing stage. Contact us for current availability or testing information. Store links will be added here when available.

Can I share an idea or suggestion?

Absolutely. Choose Feedback or idea in the form, tell us which game it is for and describe what you would love to see.

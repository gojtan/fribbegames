3. In-app purchases

Some games offer optional in-app purchases, such as removing ads, permanent boosts, cosmetics, starter packs or packs of in-game currency. All games can be played without paying. Prices are shown in your local currency in Google Play before you confirm, and Google Play handles payment, receipts and taxes.

One-time purchases, such as “Remove ads” or a permanent boost, stay with your Google account for as long as the game is offered. If you reinstall the game or switch phones, sign in with the same Google account and the game restores them automatically, or through a “Restore purchases” button where one is shown.

Consumable purchases, such as currency packs or refills, are used up in the game and cannot be restored once spent.

Where a game offers “Remove ads”, it removes the need to watch ads to receive rewards. Daily limits, cooldowns and other game rules still apply.

If a purchase does not arrive, email us with the game name and your Google Play order number (it starts with “GPA.”). Never send us card details or passwords.
